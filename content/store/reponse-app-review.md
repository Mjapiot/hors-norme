2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Hors Norme ("Off the Charts") is a French-language party game about world records. A question asks for a record ("How tall is the tallest man ever?"); each team secretly types its estimate on the same phone, passes the phone, then all answers are revealed together. The closer you are, the more points you score.
It is meant for friends and families playing together in the same room (2 to 6 teams or 2 to 10 players), ages 4+. It brings people together around a fun, educational trivia moment without needing one device per player, an account or an Internet connection.

3. HOW TO USE THE APP
No login or sample file is needed.
- Tap "Nouvelle partie" (New game).
- Step 1: choose "Jouer en équipes" (teams).
- Step 2: keep the default team names, tap "Continuer".
- Step 3: choose "5 questions".
- Step 4: keep all themes, tap "C'est parti !".
- For each question: tap "À vos réponses", type a number on the keypad, tap "Valider", hand the phone to the next team, then tap "Révéler" to see the answers, the scores and the source of the record.
- "Comment on joue ?" on the home screen opens an illustrated tutorial.
The game works offline; questions are embedded in the app.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (supabase.com): read-only database used to download updated questions when a connection is available. No user data is sent.
- Vercel (vercel.com): hosts the website and the privacy policy, support and legal pages.
No authentication service, payment processor, analytics, advertising or AI service is used in the app.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app is in French and works the same way in every region where it is available.

Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material: questions are original texts written by the developer about publicly available facts, and each answer cites its public source (e.g. Guinness World Records, official organisations, encyclopedias).
